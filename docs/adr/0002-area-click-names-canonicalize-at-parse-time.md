# Gesture names canonicalize at parse time

Reorderings of any documented gesture name, on both devices, are accepted and rewritten to the canonical name in one place, the parse-time slug lookup in `src/Config.m`, so dispatch, the menu, app-scope override matching, and skipped-line reporting see only canonical names. Most names have a unique word multiset, so a bag of words matching exactly one slug resolves to it. Three families share a multiset and need more than the bag: the edge-region family (`left-edge-top-half-click` and `top-edge-left-half-click` share one) is split by adjacency — the direction word attached to `edge` names the edge and the remaining direction and size words form the part; hold-tap names pair each direction with the `hold` or `tap` beside it; brush names (`index-to-pinky`, `pinky-to-index`) are ordered by `to`, so only their canonical spellings load. An ordering still ambiguous after these rules is rejected. Two constraints follow: a future gesture name in any family must not share a word multiset with an existing name unless an adjacency rule can split them (a check asserts this uniqueness), and any name-level feature must operate on canonical space through this parse-time chokepoint.
